# Official Release: Mission CSA-11 🚀🛰️

🌌 **MISSION CSA-11: COROLT-IV B MAIDEN LAUNCH REACHES 269 KM APOGEE, VALIDATING AUTONOMOUS ASCENT & kOS GUIDANCE AMID UPPER STAGE ANOMALY!**

The Corolt Space Agency (CSA) has conducted its historic first heavy orbital-class flight with **Mission CSA-11**, marking a giant leap forward for Kerbin's space program:

- 🚀 **Maiden Flight of Corolt-IV B**: Successfully debuted the modular heavy liquid launch architecture (`Etoh-140-TU` core + dual `Shrimp` radial boosters + `Belle-RLX81` liquid upper stage).
- 🛰️ **269.1 km Trans-Atmospheric Insertion**: Propelled the upper stage and satellite stack to an orbital speed of **1,948.2 m/s** ($6,314.8\text{ km/h}$) and reached an apogee of **269.08 km**, deep in the exosphere.
- 💻 **Autonomous kOS Flight Guidance v4.0**: Fully closed-loop autonomous navigation executed liftoff, umbilical release, gravity turn along Heading 90.0º East ($i \approx 0.09^\circ$), dynamic atmospheric throttling, core MECO at 46 km, and deterministic fairing jettison at $>55\text{ km}$.
- 🛡️ **Benign Aerodynamic Envelope**: Max dynamic pressure ($Q_{\max}$) peaked at just **17.77 kPa**, well below structural safety limits, validating the streamlined 1.25m fairing architecture.
- ⚠️ **In-Flight Propulsion Anomaly**: At $T+237.6\text{ s}$ ($132.26\text{ km}$ altitude), the upper stage `Belle-RLX81` engine experienced a mechanical breakdown (*Kerbalism Engine Breakdown*), ending the apogee push 30 km shy of the intended 300 km orbit.
- 📋 **Agency Canon & Lessons Learned**: Officially registered as an agency partial success and engineering benchmark. Empirical telemetry has isolated the premature booster separation timeout, unlocking $>350\text{ m/s}$ in bonus $\Delta v$ for upcoming launches.

🎯 **Next Mission (CSA-12)**: The sister launch vehicle, **`COROLT IVB - B2`**, is undergoing final pad preparation to fly **CoroltSat-1B** and establish the permanent anchor node of the Kerbin Comms Constellation! 🛰️🌍
