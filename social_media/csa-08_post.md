# Official Release: Mission CSA-08 ⚡💥

🛰️ **MISSION CSA-08: DEEP SPACE ASCENT TO 179 KM, TELEMETRY TRIUMPH & POWER DEPLETION LESSONS!**

The Corolt Space Agency (CSA) has conducted flight operations for **CSA-08**:
- 🌌 **Apogee in Deep Space (179.4 km)**: Boosted by the tandem RT-10 / SRM-XL stack, CSA-08 soared well into outer space, achieving the highest altitude yet in agency history.
- 🧭 **Inland Ascent Steering**: Executed autonomous pitch-kick and azimuth guidance to Heading 314º Northwest, traversing over 220 km downrange.
- 📡 **Telemetry Streams +11.3 Science Points**: Before power cutoff, onboard CommNet transmitters successfully beamed back complete scientific surveys for Low Atmosphere, Upper Atmosphere, and Low Space.
- ⚡ **The Power Anomaly**: A cumulative ~3.5 EC/s draw led by the thermal Mini-Lab and SAS reaction wheels fully exhausted the 400 EC battery pack at T+113s, preventing parachute arming and resulting in a high-speed water impact.
- 🚀 **The SpaceX Mindset**: As flight controllers often say, *"Fail fast, learn faster"*. The comprehensive telemetry gathered provided the exact engineering data needed to harden our next mission.

🎯 **Next Stop**: Mission **CSA-09** rolls out to the pad with an upgraded 800 EC battery pack, automated science duty cycling, and equatorial trajectory compensation (Heading 270º) to touch down safely on continental terrain! 🚀
