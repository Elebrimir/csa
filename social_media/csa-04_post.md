# 📢 Official Release: Mission CSA-04

**Kerbin Launch Complex — Year 1, Day 35**

The **Corolt Space Agency (CSA)** has completed mission **CSA-04**, marking one of the most intense and rewarding days in our aerospace engineering program.

🚀 **Key Mission Highlights:**
* **Corolt-IIb Baptism of Fire:** The vehicle attained a peak altitude of **14,489 meters (14.5 km)** at a maximum velocity of **1,100 km/h** above the grasslands of Kerbin.
* **Dynamic Booster Jettison:** During spin-stabilized ascent, the 4 auxiliary radial boosters cleanly separated due to centrifugal force, allowing the central core to sustain a razor-sharp vertical climb.
* **100% Payload Recovery:** The instrument capsule touched down softly under main parachute after more than 7 minutes of flight and was recovered in pristine condition.
* **A New Scientific Era:** The operational debut of the BAROTRON Barometer, 2HOT Thermometer, and meteorological suite harvested **over 10 new science points**, expanding agency research reserves to **12.95 points**.

KSC engineers are already integrating these lessons into the design of the upcoming **Corolt-III**, engineered to take the CSA flag to the edge of space.

*Ad Astra Per Scientiam.* 🛰️✨
