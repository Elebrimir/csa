# Official Release: Mission CSA-07 🌊🚀

🛰️ **MISSION CSA-07: COROLT-IIIb DEMONSTRATES CONTROL AUTHORITY & SPLASHES DOWN WITH MAJOR SCIENTIFIC HAUL!**

The Corolt Space Agency (CSA) has completed the flight test of the upgraded **Corolt-IIIb**:
- 📐 **Active Control Surfaces**: New movable Etoh-CS and Dioscuri-AFD1 fins delivered aerodynamic control torque, tilting the trajectory decisively toward the East.
- 🌊 **The Great Eastward Leap (>315 km Downrange)**: The stack traversed over 30 degrees of longitude across Kerbin, building **1,581 m/s orbital velocity** and reaching **138.6 km apogee**.
- 🔬 **Conquering Ocean Biomes**: First splashdown in agency history, yielding new marine biomes data and adding +9.8 science points (career total: **75.76 points** in R&D).
- 🪂 **Recovery to VAB Warehouse**: Following a descent under parachute at 0.1 m/s water impact, the capsule was recovered intact directly into the VAB warehouse, ready for rapid reuse!
- 🛡️ **Engineering Resilience**: Resolving a real-time scripting event produced the new autonomous contingency routine (`emergency_recovery.ks`).

🎯 **Next Stop**: Mission CSA-08 will re-fly the recovered airframe with fresh scientific modules under fully autonomous guidance! 🚀
