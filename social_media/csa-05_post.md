# 📢 Official Release: Mission CSA-05

**Kerbin Launch Complex — Year 1, Day 45**

The **Corolt Space Agency (CSA)** proudly announces the success of mission **CSA-05**, crossing into the **Upper Atmosphere (`FlyingHigh`)** of Kerbin for the first time with our new launcher, **Corolt-III**.

🚀 **Key Mission Highlights:**
* **Corolt-III Inline Debut:** The new two-stage tandem vehicle exhibited flawless aerodynamics during ascent, exceeding Mach 1.5 in a straight vertical climb.
* **New Historical Altitude Record:** Despite an upper-stage ignition anomaly at 13.7 km, the ballistic momentum of the RT-10 «Hammer» lower stage catapulted the vehicle to an apogee of **20,432 meters (20.43 km)**!
* **Pioneering High-Altitude Science:** Aeronomy, barometric pressure, and meteorology sensors captured upper atmosphere data before filling onboard memory.
* **100% Recovery at KSC:** After nearly 13 minutes of flight, the instrument capsule touched down smoothly under parachute just meters from the Space Center runway.
* **Scientific Balance:** Payload recovery added **+7.14 net science points**, bringing CSA reserves to **10.09 points**.

The engineering team has prepared the second Corolt-III unit with expanded 32 MB avionics storage for mission **CSA-05b**, targeting 50 km!
