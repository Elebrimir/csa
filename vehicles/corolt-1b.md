## 💡 Improvements over the original Corolt-I

1. **Tripled Electrical Capacity**:
   - Integrated three battery modules to ensure computer storage and Kerbalism environmental sensors maintain continuous data transmission until ground contact without power depletion.
2. **Flight Stability (4-Fin Cruciform Setup)**:
   - Transitioned from 3 to 4 radial fins to eliminate aerodynamic precession and rolling torque during peak acceleration.
3. **Delayed Deployment Descent Profile**:
   - Parachute deployment calibrated below 2,000 m AGL to eliminate prolonged drift and optimize landing footprint.
