## 📝 Flight Summary
Mission **CSA-03** marked a milestone for the Corolt Space Agency with the inaugural flight of the multi-stage **Corolt-II**. 

The 0.625m SRM-XL booster propelled the stack steadily for 58 seconds past 10,600 meters altitude. Staging through the `SR.Decoupler` explosive ring triggered cleanly, followed immediately by upper SRM-L motor ignition. This second impulse in the thin stratosphere accelerated the probe to **401.6 m/s (Mach 1.2+)**, vaulting the payload to an apogee of **23,968 meters**.

Following ballistic return, the parachute deployed at 2,500 m and the payload touched down softly on the grasslands, enabling recovery of scientific telemetry.
